# My Favorite Series: The Amazing Digital Circus

##Overview
**The Amazing Digital Circus** is an indie animated dark comedy and psychological drama created by Gooseworkx and produced by Glitch Productions. The plot follows six characters trapped in a virtual reality world where an eccentric AI forces them to go on wacky adventures to keep them from losing their minds and get abstracted.

---

### Why I Love It
* **Contrasting Tone:** Combines bright, nostalgic 90s CGI aesthetics with dark humor.
* **Amazing Cast:** Every character copes with their trapped realities in distinct, relatable, and chaotic ways.
* **Indie Animation:** Expressive animations, sharp comedic timing, and amazing voice acting.
